Staff performance counts referrals each staff member reviewed.

A referral is included only when it has a reviewer (reviewed_by_user_id). That user is the staff name.

For each staff member:
- Reviewed = number of referrals they reviewed
- Approved = those referrals now queued, completed, missed, or archived
- Rejected = those referrals with status rejected

The Staff performance chart shows Reviewed, and only the top 8 staff.
